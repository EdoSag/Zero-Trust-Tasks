Zero-Trust Tasks keeps your to-dos, errands and plans in one simple list, with reminders that arrive on time. Every task is encrypted on your phone, so your plans stay yours.

WHAT IT DOES
• Several lists with their own colours; move tasks between them.
• Subtasks: drag a task onto another to indent it, and drag to reorder.
• Star the important ones and see them all in one Starred view.
• Quick add in plain words: "call mum tomorrow 6pm" (English and Hebrew).
• Reminders with Complete and Snooze buttons right on the notification.
• Location reminders: get reminded when you arrive or leave a place. Your location never leaves the phone.
• Repeating tasks and due dates that show up in Zero-Trust Calendar.
• Link a task to a note or a contact in the other Zero-Trust apps.
• Shared lists with family or friends who use Zero-Trust, still end-to-end encrypted.
• Home-screen widget: see your list, tick off tasks and add new ones.
• Import from Google Tasks (Google Takeout).

WHY ZERO-TRUST
• End-to-end encrypted: everything is encrypted on your phone before it syncs. We can't read it, and neither can anyone who breaks into the server.
• One private account for every Zero-Trust app, unlocked with your fingerprint or face.
• Works offline and syncs across your devices when you're back online.
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no data sold. Ever.
• Open source (AGPL-3.0), so anyone can check what the app does.
• English and Hebrew, with full right-to-left support.

Part of the Zero-Trust suite: Notes, Tasks, Calendar, Contacts, Phone, Fitness, Meds, Time and Browser. One account opens them all.
